1. LOCAL-FIRST DELETION ARCHITECTURE
Meowoirs operates on a 100% local storage architecture. When you delete a memory or cat profile using in-app actions, the record is immediately and permanently removed from your device's local database.

2. CAT DELETION SPECIFICS
Deleting a cat profile removes the cat from your collection, but associated journal memories remain intact in your memory archive to prevent accidental loss of user stories.

3. PERMANENT LOSS & NO CLOUD RESTORATION
LIMANEX does not operate remote servers or cloud backups. Once records are deleted on the device, or if app storage is cleared via Android Settings, data cannot be recovered by LIMANEX. Users are responsible for independent external backups.
