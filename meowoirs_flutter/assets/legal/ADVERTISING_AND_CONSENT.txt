1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs is provided as a free application supported by digital advertising.
To maintain the app, ensure continuous compatibility with modern Android
releases, and cover ongoing development costs, Meowoirs displays advertisements
served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

This document explains how advertising operates in Meowoirs, how consent is
managed, how you can exercise your privacy rights, and the technologies used.

3. ADVERTISING TYPES & USER EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs utilizes two standard, non-disruptive ad formats:

3.1. Banner Advertisements:
Small banner advertisements may appear at designated layout positions, such as
the bottom of selected screens, designed to avoid obstructing user journaling
tasks.

3.2. Interstitial Advertisements:
Full-screen interstitial advertisements may occasionally appear at appropriate
natural transition points according to the application's configured ad frequency
rules. Interstitial advertisements are not intentionally presented while the
user is actively filling out a form, typing content, selecting media, or
performing a critical save/delete confirmation action. These ads include a
standard close ("X") button provided by Google's SDK.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
Meowoirs does NOT currently include rewarded video ads, paid ad-free purchases,
or recurring subscriptions.

4. GOOGLE ADVERTISING ID & TECHNICAL TELEMETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on your device configuration and regional regulations, Google AdMob
may process:
- Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): A resettable pseudonymous identifier
  provided by Android used for ad attribution, frequency capping, and ad
  relevance.
- Coarse IP Address: Used by Google to determine approximate country or city
  for ad localization and click fraud prevention.
- Technical Interaction Data: Ad impression timestamps, clicks, and rendering
  metrics.

STRICT ISOLATION GUARANTEE:
Your personal cat profiles, journal notes, pet photos, and mood tags are strictly
isolated from AdMob. LIMANEX does not provide or sell your journal contents to
advertisers or ad networks.

5. CONSENT MANAGEMENT & USER MESSAGING PLATFORM (UMP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consent requirements differ significantly around the world. In jurisdictions
such as the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland,
applicable regulations require explicit user consent before storing or accessing
information on a device (e.g., using identifiers) and before serving
personalized advertisements.

To comply with these legal requirements, Meowoirs incorporates Google's User
Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, adhering to the IAB Europe Transparency and
Consent Framework (TCF v2.2):
- First Launch Detection: The SDK evaluates your geographic location and local
  legal requirements to display a certified consent form where mandatory.
- Consent Choices: You may consent to personalized advertising, choose
  non-personalized ads, or configure individual vendor options.
- Non-Personalized Ads: If you decline personalized advertising, ads will still
  be shown, but they will be based on contextual information (such as general
  app context) rather than past user behavior or ad profiles.

6. HOW TO MANAGE OR REVOKE YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are always in control of your advertising choices:

6.1. In-App Privacy Options (Settings):
You can review, change, or revoke your consent preferences at any time by
navigating within Meowoirs to:
Settings -> "Gizlilik Tercihleri" (Privacy Options)
Tapping this option will reopen the official Google UMP consent dialogue,
allowing you to adjust your choices.

6.2. Android Operating System Controls:
You can manage or delete your advertising identifier at the system level on
your Android device:
- Navigate to Android Settings -> Google -> Ads (or Privacy -> Ads).
- Select "Reset advertising ID" to generate a new pseudonymous identifier.
- Select "Delete advertising ID" to prevent apps from accessing your GAID.
- Toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" where supported by your OS version.

7. REGIONAL PRIVACY NOTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1. EEA & UK (GDPR):
Legal basis for ad personalization and identifier use is user consent obtained
via UMP. Non-personalized advertising relies on legitimate interest for fraud
prevention and basic frequency capping as defined under Google's policies.

7.2. United States (California / CCPA / CPRA & Other State Laws):
Meowoirs does not sell personal information in exchange for monetary payment.
Where the use of advertising identifiers is classified as "sharing" for
cross-context behavioral advertising, you can opt out via your Android device's
privacy settings or the in-app Privacy Options.
