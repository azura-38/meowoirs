1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs is built on a local-first architectural principle. The core journaling
functionality—storing cat profiles, memories, notes, photos, and moods—operates
entirely offline within your device's internal SQLite/Room database. LIMANEX
does not operate any remote database or cloud server to receive, process, or
store your private journal entries.

However, to provide crash diagnostics and support app development via
advertising, Meowoirs integrates verified third-party Software Development
Kits (SDKs) provided by Google LLC and its affiliates. This document provides
a transparent, detailed breakdown of these third-party services.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STRICT SEPARATION OF JOURNAL DATA AND TECHNICAL METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs maintains a strict boundary between user content and third-party SDKs:

A. LOCAL USER JOURNAL DATA (NOT SHARED):
Your cat names, diary notes, reflections, mood choices, photo file references,
and pet milestones remain strictly on your Android device. They are NOT
uploaded to LIMANEX, NOT indexed by Google, and NOT logged into Crashlytics.

B. THIRD-PARTY TECHNICAL & ADVERTISING METRICS:
Third-party SDKs handle automated network communication solely for technical
stability analysis, consent verification, and advertising delivery as detailed
below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FIREBASE CRASHLYTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provider: Google LLC / Firebase
Purpose: Application stability, crash reporting, bug detection, and diagnostics.

Meowoirs integrates Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application reliability.
When an unhandled exception or critical application crash occurs, Crashlytics
automatically generates a technical crash report.

Technical Diagnostics Collected:
- Stack traces and execution failure logs.
- Device make, model, architecture, and hardware characteristics.
- Android Operating System version and build number.
- Meowoirs application version and build number.
- System orientation, free disk space, and memory state at the moment of crash.
- Timestamp of the crash event.

Explicit Safeguard:
Meowoirs does NOT intentionally log, attach, or send your cat names, journal
entry text, notes, mood selections, or local photo URIs to Firebase Crashlytics.

Crashlytics does collect technical diagnostic and device telemetry to help
developers fix software defects; it does not access or inspect your personal
journal entries.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase Data Processing Terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms

4. GOOGLE USER MESSAGING PLATFORM (UMP) SDK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provider: Google LLC
Purpose: Privacy compliance, cookie and consent management, IAB TCF 2.2 framework.

Meowoirs uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage privacy
consent in compliance with international legal frameworks, including the
European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and relevant
digital advertising guidelines.

Functionality:
- Presents consent forms to users located in jurisdictions requiring consent
  before loading personalized advertising.
- Transmits consent string status (e.g., TCF string) to ad delivery networks.
- Enables users to revisit and adjust their consent preferences at any time
  via the in-app "Gizlilik Tercihleri" (Privacy Options) button located in
  Settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

- IP address (used by Google for general geographic area routing and fraud
  prevention).
- Diagnostic interaction metrics (ad impressions, clicks, load performance).

Ad Formats & Official Identifiers:

Ad Personalization Control:
Users can opt out of personalized ads or delete/reset their Advertising ID
at any time through Android Settings -> Google -> Ads. Where regional consent

Google Ads Policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
