1. INTRODUCTION & LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs is designed and operated by LIMANEX as a private, local-first companion
journal application for cat owners.

Unlike cloud-dependent services, Meowoirs does not require an account, does not
provide a login system, and does not synchronize your journal data to any remote
server operated by LIMANEX. All personal journal entries, including cat profiles,
names, photos, mood tags, and notes, reside solely within your Android device's
internal storage and local SQLite/Room database.

Because LIMANEX does not possess, host, or transmit a copy of your personal
journal content, your control over your data is immediate, local, and direct.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO DELETE DATA WITHIN MEOWOIRS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Users can delete content directly within the application interface:

2.1. Deleting a Memory Entry:
Users can delete any individual memory entry by navigating to the memory detail
or list view, selecting the delete option, and confirming the prompt. When
confirmed, the memory record is permanently deleted from the local Room
database on the device.

2.2. Deleting a Cat Profile (Specific Database Behavior):
Users can delete an existing cat profile from the Cat Profile screen.
IMPORTANT CURRENT DATABASE BEHAVIOR:
In accordance with Meowoirs' current database design, deleting a cat profile
removes the cat's profile record from the local database. However, this action
does NOT intentionally delete the memory records associated with that cat.
Existing memories remain intact in the local journal to prevent accidental
loss of user-created journal memories. If you wish to delete both the cat and
the associated memories, you must delete the individual memories separately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PERMANENT DATA LOSS & ABSENCE OF CLOUD RECOVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT NOTICE ON PERMANENT DATA LOSS:
When a record (such as a memory or cat profile) is deleted in Meowoirs, the
deletion is executed immediately in the local SQLite database.

BECAUSE LIMANEX DOES NOT MAINTAIN A REMOTE BACKUP OR CLOUD COPY OF YOUR JOURNAL
CONTENT, LIMANEX CANNOT RESTORE, RECOVER, OR RETRIEVE CONTENT ONCE DELETED FROM
YOUR DEVICE.

Deleted content should be considered permanently lost. LIMANEX customer support
cannot assist with recovering deleted memories or profiles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CLEARING APP DATA, UNINSTALLING & OPERATING SYSTEM BACKUP BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meowoirs does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization service.
Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may remove locally
stored Meowoirs data. Android or device-level backup and restoration behavior,
where available, is controlled by the operating system and device configuration
and is not operated or guaranteed by LIMANEX.

4.1. Clearing App Storage via Android Settings:
If you navigate to your device's Android Settings -> Apps -> Meowoirs -> Storage
and select "Clear Data" or "Clear Storage", the Android operating system may
erase all local app storage, including your SQLite/Room database and local
preferences.

4.2. Uninstalling the Application:
Uninstalling Meowoirs from your Android device typically prompts the operating
system to delete the application's private internal storage sandbox, which
contains the local database and settings. Any subsequent data recovery or
re-installation restore depends exclusively on Android system-level settings and
device configurations outside LIMANEX's control.

4.3. Device Loss, Damage, or Factory Reset:
If your device is lost, damaged, corrupted, or undergoes a factory reset,
locally stored Meowoirs content may be lost unless you have maintained your
own independent external backups.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. USER RESPONSIBILITY FOR INDEPENDENT BACKUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are solely responsible for maintaining independent copies or backups of any
irreplaceable journal text, notes, or photographs. Meowoirs does not offer an
automated cloud backup service.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. LIMITS OF TECHNICAL DATA DELETION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When records are deleted in Meowoirs, standard database and file system delete
operations are executed. Meowoirs does not claim universal cryptographic or
forensic-grade sanitization of every possible device hardware block or temporary
operating system cache. Operating system wear-leveling, temporary flash caches,
or low-level hardware memory management are governed by your device's operating
system and hardware vendor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THIRD-PARTY TECHNICAL DIAGNOSTICS & ADVERTISING DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technical telemetry collected by integrated third-party SDKs (such as Google
Mobile Ads / UMP and Firebase Crashlytics) consists of technical diagnostics,
device crash reports, and advertising identifiers as permitted by user consent.
These third-party services do not store your local cat names, journal text, or
notes. Deletion of advertising identifiers can be managed at any time via your
device's Google Play Services settings (Reset or Delete Advertising ID) and
consent can be updated via the in-app "Gizlilik Tercihleri" (Privacy Options) menu.
